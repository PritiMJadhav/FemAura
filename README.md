# FemAura 🌸✨

## 📖 Abstract
**FemAura** is a wellness application designed to empower women in managing the physical and emotional challenges associated with menstruation, pregnancy, and hormonal changes[cite: 45]. The app provides a safe, supportive space where users can address mood swings, menstrual cramps, stress, and other wellness concerns through evidence-based tools and features[cite: 45]. By blending technology with empathy, FemAura serves as a digital companion dedicated to supporting women’s health and emotional well-being in all stages of life[cite: 45].

---

## 🌟 Key Features
* **Guided Meditation & Yoga:** Access to yoga poses (supported by GIF or animated visuals) and guided meditation to promote physical relaxation[cite: 45].
* **Calming Music Playlists:** Curated audio tracks designed to help reduce stress and ease anxiety[cite: 45].
* **Hydration & Wellness Reminders:** Smart reminders to help users stay hydrated and maintain consistent self-care habits[cite: 45].
* **Daily Self-Care Tips:** Actionable tips to support daily mental and physical health[cite: 45].
* **Interactive Mood Tracker:** A dedicated tool allowing users to monitor and track emotional well-being over time[cite: 45].

## 🚀 Application Design & Goals
* **Holistic Approach:** Focuses on both emotional and physical health, addressing challenges like menstrual cramps and stress[cite: 45].
* **Scalable & Efficient:** Built to ensure high performance, easy scalability, and accessibility for everyday users[cite: 45].
